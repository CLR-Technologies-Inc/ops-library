# Six Sigma roles and belts

Six Sigma runs on a defined set of roles. The belt names come from martial-arts ranks. They give people a career path in improvement work across every function and level. Before Six Sigma, quality work stayed mostly on the production floor and with statisticians in a separate department.

| Role | Who | Time on Six Sigma | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Executive Leadership** | CEO and top management | Part-time, strategic | Set the vision. Give people the freedom and resources to improve. Break down barriers between departments and resistance to change. |
| **Champion** | Upper management | Part-time | Own the rollout across the organization. Select and sponsor projects, run tollgate reviews, and mentor Black Belts. |
| **Master Black Belt (MBB)** | Selected by Champions | Full-time | Act as in-house coach and expert. Support Champions, guide Black and Green Belts, and keep methods consistent across departments. |
| **Black Belt (BB)** | Trained practitioners under an MBB | Full-time | Lead complex, cross-functional projects and special tasks. Apply the statistical tools. |
| **Green Belt (GB)** | Employees in their regular roles | Part-time, alongside their job | Run smaller projects or work on Black Belt projects, under BB guidance. |
| **Yellow Belt** (optional) | Team members | Occasional | Know the basic tools. Take part in projects and gather data. |
| **White Belt** (optional) | Any employee | Awareness only | Know the concepts. Usually not on a project team. |

Some organizations add other levels, such as an Orange Belt between Yellow and Green.

## Certification

- There is **no single governing body** for Six Sigma certification. Criteria vary widely, and some companies require only a course and one project.
- Motorola and GE built the first internal programs. Many organizations began offering certificates in the 1990s.
- **ASQ (American Society for Quality)** Black Belt certification requires a written exam and a signed affidavit. The candidate must have completed two projects, or one project plus three years of experience in the body of knowledge.
- In 2008, Motorola University worked with Vative and the Lean Six Sigma Society of Professionals to develop comparable Lean certification standards.
- **ISO 13053-1 and 13053-2 (2011)** define the DMAIC method and its tools, including competence expectations for each role.

**Practical guidance:** Judge practitioners by projects they completed and verified, not by certificates alone. Critics have noted a large training industry with uneven quality.

## Sizing the program

| Organization | Typical structure |
| --- | --- |
| Large enterprise | Executive steering group, Champions in each business unit, MBBs at a central office, full-time BBs, and many GBs |
| Mid-size (about 500 people or fewer) | One Champion, one or two BBs (possibly part-time or outside coaches), and Green Belts in each area |
| Small shop | The owner or manager acts as Champion, one trained GB or BB leads, and everyone learns the White/Yellow Belt basics |
