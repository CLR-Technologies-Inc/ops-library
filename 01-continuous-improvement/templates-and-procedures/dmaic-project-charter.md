**Problem statement.** What is wrong, where, since when, how big is it, and what does it cost? Give no cause and no solution.

**Goal statement.** Use SMART terms: specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound.

> Example: Cut Line 3 scrap to 1.5% or less by 30 June 2026 and hold it there for 3 months.
